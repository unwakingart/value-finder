Value Finder

How light or dark is it? Move the ring over any part of a photo to read its value, then see how to mix it.

Free to use. Your photo stays on your device: nothing is uploaded.

Open a photo, drop one here, or paste it (Ctrl or ⌘ + V).
Your photo stays on your computer: nothing is uploaded.

Gray ruler: drag it over the photo and find the hole where the photo and the gray around it look equally light. Squint if it helps.
Hover to read a spot. Click to pin it, drag a pin to move it, and name your pins in the list. A bigger ring averages out small details like ripples or texture.
Warm and cool: warm colors turn orange, cool colors blue, neutral ones stay gray. Color strength: dull colors turn gray and strong colors keep their color.
